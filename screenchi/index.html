Take back your screen time

Screenchi turns “staying under a daily app limit” into a streak you’ll actually want to keep. Pick an app, set a limit, and watch your days go green.

Build the habit, not the guilt

🎯

Set a daily limit

Pick an app and choose a goal from 15 minutes to 1½ hours. That’s the whole setup.

🟢

Build a streak

Every day under your limit earns a green dot. Go over and it turns red — simple, visual, motivating.

⏱️

Real-time alerts

A gentle heads-up as you approach your limit, and a nudge the moment you reach it. No surprises.

🔒

Private by design

Screenchi never learns which app you track — Apple keeps that private. Everything stays on your device.

🧩

Home Screen widgets

Keep your streak and this week’s dots one glance away, right on your Home Screen.

😽

Calm, not naggy

A friendly design that celebrates progress instead of shaming the slip-ups.
